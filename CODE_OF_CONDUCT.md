# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming project for everyone.
Participants should use inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints,
accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and
show empathy toward others.

Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, publication of private information, and
other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate are unacceptable. Maintainers
may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
ban participants for behavior they deem harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security/contact facilities. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly.

This short form follows the intent of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
